how it works

method.

the rule of three — no shortcuts, no wasted turns, the same three steps for every subject, every time.

most tutoring marks a wrong answer incorrect and moves on, or re-shows the exact explanation you already didn't get. arthic runs every wrong answer through the same three steps instead.

  1. 01

    identify

    find the actual mistake, not just the wrong answer

    figures out exactly where your understanding broke — a formula, a definition, a step in the logic — instead of just marking it incorrect.

  2. 02

    explain

    a concept explained the way a friend would

    a plain-language explanation with a local, relatable example — a circuit through load-shedding, probability through cricket stats — until it clicks.

  3. 03

    test

    a next challenge, not just a pat on the back

    one more question, calibrated to be just hard enough, built to check the concept actually stuck before moving on.

an example: miss a projectile-motion numerical for the third time, and arthic works backward through your last attempt instead of re-showing the formula — maybe you're resolving the wrong velocity component, maybe you're misreading the angle. once it finds which one, it explains that exact piece and hands you a new problem built to need the same understanding, different numbers.
