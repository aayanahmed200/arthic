QUESTIONS

What is ready today?

Can I use Arthic's practice session now?

Yes. The sample session is available and includes original questions across all four exam tracks. It is not an official past-paper bank.

Is the diagnostic live?

No. Diagnostic assessments are still in development.

Can I save a study plan or progress?

Not yet. Personal study planning and saved progress are planned features.

Are the questions official past papers?

No. The current questions are original examples written for Arthic. Check the relevant examining body for official test information.
