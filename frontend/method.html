how it works

method.

the rule of three — no shortcuts, no wasted turns, the same three steps for every subject, every time.

most tutoring, human or software, does one of two things when you get something wrong: it marks it incorrect and moves on, or it re-shows you the same explanation you already didn't understand. arthic's mentor is built to do neither. every wrong answer, every "I don't get this," runs through the same three-step loop before the mentor moves anywhere else.

  1. 01

    identify

    find the actual mistake, not just the wrong answer

    most students re-read the same explanation when they get something wrong. arthic starts by figuring out exactly where your understanding broke — a formula, a definition, a step in the logic — instead of just marking it incorrect.

  2. 02

    explain

    a concept explained the way a friend would

    every concept gets a plain-language explanation and a local, relatable example — a circuit explained through the load-shedding schedule you already know, a probability question through cricket stats — until it actually clicks, not just until you nod along.

  3. 03

    test

    a next challenge, not just a pat on the back

    once a concept clicks, arthic doesn't just move to the next topic — it gives you one more question built to test whether it actually stuck, calibrated to be just hard enough.

here's what that looks like on an actual question: say you miss a projectile-motion numerical for the third time this week. arthic doesn't just show you the formula again — it works backward through your last attempt to find where the logic actually broke. maybe you're reaching for the right equation but resolving the wrong component of velocity, or maybe the mechanics are fine and you're just misreading which angle the question means. once it knows which one it is, it explains that exact piece, checks that it landed, and hands you a new problem built to need the same piece of understanding — different numbers, same gap, so getting it right actually means something.

the same loop runs whether you're stuck on a Physics numerical, an English comprehension passage, or a CSS essay outline — and whether it's the first time you've asked or the fifth. one method, applied consistently, is the whole bet arthic is making against generic one-size-fits-all prep.
