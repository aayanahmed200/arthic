What is ready today?

Can I use Arthic's practice session now?

Yes. The sample session is available on this preview. It uses three original demonstration questions, not official past papers or a full question bank.

Is the diagnostic live?

No. The diagnostic is part of the planned experience and is not available yet.

Can I save a study plan or progress?

Not yet. The planner, student accounts and saved progress are still in development.

Is the AI mentor available?

No. AI-assisted explanations are planned for a later stage. The current demo uses written explanations for its original questions.
