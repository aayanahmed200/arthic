privacy.

arthic is a small, early-stage team, not a data company. This page says exactly what we collect, why, and what we do with it — no filler, no "may share with partners."

what we collect

Only what you hand us directly, through one of two forms on this site:

work email address — if you join the waitlist for early access. name, links, and whatever you write — job applications only. We don't ask for anything else, and we don't buy or import data about you from anywhere else.

what we don't do

No advertising cookies, no third-party trackers, no analytics pixels from ad networks. The fonts on this site are shipped with the site itself rather than loaded from Google Fonts or similar, so your visit isn't logged by a font CDN either. If that ever changes — say, we turn on basic, cookieless traffic analytics to see which pages people read — we'll update this page and, if it ever involves cookies, ask first.

where it lives

Form submissions are stored in a Postgres database we run on Vercel's infrastructure. Nobody outside arthic has access to it. It isn't sold, rented, or shared with advertisers, data brokers, or anyone else — the only reason we keep it is to send you the thing you asked for (a heads-up when arthic is ready, or a reply to your application).

how long we keep it

Waitlist emails stay until you ask us to remove them or unsubscribe. Job applications are kept for as long as the role stays open, plus a short window afterward in case it reopens, then deleted.

your choices

Email hello@arthic.tech and ask us to show you what we have on file, correct it, or delete it outright — we'll handle it by hand, since we're small enough that "by hand" is still realistic. Every waitlist email we send also has a one-click unsubscribe.

children

arthic isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, email us and we'll remove it.

changes to this page

If how we handle data changes in a way that matters, we'll update this page and move the date at the top. We won't backdate a change to cover something we already did.
