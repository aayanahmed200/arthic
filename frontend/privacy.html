Privacy policy.

A practical explanation of what information Arthic handles, why it is needed, which providers help us run the service, and the choices available to you.

This policy applies to arthic.tech, its practice preview, waitlist and hiring forms, and the optional Google sign-in and student workspace when enabled.

This Privacy Policy explains how the team operating Arthic ("Arthic", "we", "us" or "our") handles personal information when you use our website and study services. Arthic is the service name used at arthic.tech. For privacy questions or requests, contact hello@arthic.tech.

1. Information we collect

Information you submit directly

  • Waitlist: your email address and the time your request is received.
  • Job applications: your name, email address, the role, any link you choose to provide, your message, and optional availability or start-window information.
  • Support messages: the email address and details you include when contacting us.

Google sign-in and a student workspace

When the account feature is enabled and you choose to continue with Google, our authentication provider receives the sign-in request. We receive the account identifier, verified email address and basic profile information that Google makes available through the requested sign-in scope, such as your display name. We do not request access to your Google Drive, Gmail contents, contacts or other Google files.

If you activate a student workspace, we store the account record and the learning activity needed to provide syncing: exam track and subject, practice-session start and completion times, question identifiers, selected answer options, whether an answer was correct, and the totals needed to display your progress. We may also store the preferences you save in Settings. We do not need your date of birth to provide the current workspace.

Information collected for security and service operation

Our hosting, database and authentication services may process technical request data such as IP address, request time, device and browser information, diagnostic logs and security events. These records help deliver the site, prevent abuse, investigate faults and protect accounts.

2. Where information comes from

Information comes from you when you fill in a form, contact us, sign in, save a preference or answer a practice question. When you choose Google sign-in, Google and our authentication provider return the limited identity information needed to authenticate you. Our hosting and infrastructure providers generate technical logs as part of operating the service.

We do not buy personal data, build advertising profiles, or intentionally obtain student information from data brokers. Do not submit another person's information unless you have permission and a lawful reason to do so.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the website, waitlist, recruitment forms, sign-in and requested account features.
  • To check answers, provide explanations, sync your practice history and calculate progress you ask to see.
  • To remember settings you choose, keep your account secure and respond to support or privacy requests.
  • To prevent spam, fraud, misuse, unauthorised access and attacks on the service.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot and improve Arthic, using aggregated or limited operational information where appropriate.
  • To comply with applicable law and respond to valid legal requests.

We do not currently use third-party advertising trackers or sell personal information. We do not currently run third-party analytics or behavioural advertising on the public site. If that changes, we will update this policy and introduce any notices or choices required by applicable law before enabling the new processing.

4. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share information only where reasonably needed to run Arthic, respond to you, protect the service or comply with law. This can include:

  • Supabase: authentication services and, where configured, the PostgreSQL database used for account and practice records.
  • Vercel: website hosting, serverless functions, delivery and related operational security.
  • Google: authentication when you choose Continue with Google. Google's own privacy terms also apply to Google's handling of your information.
  • Professional advisers and authorities: where necessary for legal, security or regulatory purposes.

We do not give these providers permission to use Arthic student records for their own advertising. Each provider may process limited information under its own service terms and privacy documentation. We do not claim that every provider or transfer is covered by a particular certification or contract unless that arrangement has been confirmed.

Information may also be transferred as part of a genuine restructuring or transfer of the service, subject to appropriate safeguards and any notice required by law.

5. Cookies and browser storage

The public pages do not currently use advertising cookies, analytics cookies or retargeting pixels. The student sign-in flow stores its short-lived access and refresh credentials in browser sessionStorage for the active tab so that authenticated requests can be made and the session can be refreshed. Signing out removes this local session. The service also uses essential server-side authentication and security checks. See the Cookie and browser-storage notice for the separate, detailed explanation.

Fonts are bundled and served with the site from our own web assets using Fontsource packages; the current pages do not need to request the site's typefaces from Google Fonts. When you leave Arthic to sign in with Google, Google may use its own cookies and technologies on Google's domains, subject to Google's policies.

6. How long we keep information

We keep information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, to operate the service, to resolve disputes, to enforce agreements or to meet legal obligations. In particular:

  • Waitlist records: until you ask us to remove your address or the early-access programme is closed and the record is no longer needed.
  • Job applications: for the recruitment process and a reasonable period afterwards to document the process and handle related questions or claims.
  • Student accounts and practice history: while the account remains active, unless you request deletion or a record must be retained for a permitted legal or security reason.
  • Technical logs: according to the retention periods and operational controls of the relevant hosting or infrastructure provider.
  • Privacy requests: enough information to verify and respond to the request and record that it was handled.

Backups and security logs may remain for a limited period after a record is removed from the active database. Where deletion is requested, we will explain any material legal or technical reason why some information cannot immediately be removed.

7. Security

We use reasonable safeguards intended to protect information, including encrypted connections, server-side access checks, rate limiting, parameterised database queries and restricted access to administrative functions. Access to account and administrative data is limited to authorised purposes. No website, database or online transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Please use a device you trust, keep it protected, and sign out on shared devices.

If you believe your account or information has been accessed without permission, contact hello@arthic.tech promptly and include enough detail for us to investigate. Do not email passwords, access tokens or other secret credentials.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You may also withdraw a permission where processing relies on that permission. These rights are subject to identity checks, applicable exceptions and legal retention obligations.

To make a request, email hello@arthic.tech from the email address associated with the record where possible. Signed-in students may use the account's data-request controls when available. We may ask for reasonable information to confirm that the request is yours. We will respond within the period required by applicable law; we do not promise that every request can be completed instantly.

You may stop using the site, sign out, or ask to be removed from the waitlist. Deleting a browser session does not itself delete records already stored on our servers.

9. Children and young users

Arthic is designed for exam candidates, some of whom may be under 18. The service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly invite children under 13 to create accounts. Users aged 13–17 must involve a parent or legal guardian and obtain their permission before creating an account or submitting personal information. Do not use Arthic to submit another student's information or sensitive personal details that are not needed for study.

The account onboarding declaration is a self-attestation; it is not an independent verification of a parent's identity or consent. Before wider account access for minors, Arthic must complete an age-appropriate consent and support process. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate permission, contact us at hello@arthic.tech.

10. International processing and third-party links

Arthic is operated for users in Pakistan, but our service providers may host or process information in other countries. Where that happens, we take reasonable steps to use providers and safeguards suitable for the information involved and the laws that apply. We cannot control the privacy practices of external websites linked from Arthic; review their policies before providing information to them.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the service or applicable requirements change. The date at the top shows when the current version was last updated. For a material change affecting existing accounts, we will provide notice through the site or another reasonable channel where required.

For privacy questions, access, correction, export or deletion requests, contact hello@arthic.tech. Please do not send passwords, one-time codes or access tokens by email.

This policy describes Arthic's intended handling of information and is not a certification, legal opinion or guarantee that every legal requirement has been met. Applicable law may give you rights that are not described here.
