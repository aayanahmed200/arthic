careers

small team.
open to the right people.

  1. 01

    developers

    game developers

    Designers, artists, or generalists who want real ownership over a small, focused project instead of a cog role on a big one. Engine-agnostic experience is fine — taste and follow-through matter more than a specific toolset.

    apply for this role

  2. 02

    ambassadors

    community ambassadors

    People already talking about small, independent games — on YouTube, forums, wherever — who want to help us find the players who'd actually love what we're making, without turning it into marketing-speak.

    apply for this role

  3. 03

    engineering

    software engineers

    Help build and maintain the tools around the games themselves — this site, the backend, our internal pipeline. Full-stack leanings welcome; we care more about simple, maintainable systems than about a particular stack.

    apply for this role

not quite a fit?

apply anyway.

If none of the three roles above are exactly right, use whichever one is closest and tell us what you'd rather be doing — we're small enough to figure it out from there.
