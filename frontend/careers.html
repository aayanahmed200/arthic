01
Subject guidance · LCAT / NET / CSS / MDCAT

Student Mentor

Help other students work through difficult concepts and questions. A mentor makes the reasoning easier to follow, helps a learner spot a gap and suggests a sensible next topic to practise.

What you could do
  • Explain one topic using clear, worked examples.
  • Lead a small, structured revision session.
  • Review a student's practice mistakes and suggest what to revisit.
  • Share feedback on whether Arthic explanations are accurate and easy to follow.
What helps
  • Strong understanding of a subject or exam track.
  • Recent experience preparing for the relevant exam.
  • Patient communication and a willingness to check facts.
  • A short sample explanation or teaching exercise during selection.

Mentor matching and paid session availability are planned, not currently live.

02
Learning material · Original questions

Student Contributor

Turn your strongest subjects into useful study material. Contributors help build original questions, concise topic notes and explanations that teach the steps behind an answer, rather than asking students to memorise a letter.

What you could create
  • Original MCQs for a specific topic and exam track.
  • Worked solutions with clear, ordered reasoning.
  • Short concept notes, definitions and formula reminders.
  • Corrections and feedback on draft learning material.
What helps
  • Accurate subject knowledge and careful writing.
  • Ability to explain why an option is right or wrong.
  • Attention to syllabus relevance and question quality.
  • Original work or content you have permission to use.

Do not submit copied paid question-bank content, book extracts or past-paper material without permission. Sample work may be reviewed for accuracy before acceptance.

03
Campus community · Student outreach

Student Ambassador

Help Arthic listen to the students it is meant to serve. Ambassadors introduce the project in their campus or study community, support small demos and bring back honest feedback about what learners need most.

What you could do
  • Share approved product updates with classmates and societies.
  • Help organise a small introduction or feedback session.
  • Collect student suggestions and recurring preparation challenges.
  • Represent Arthic respectfully and avoid spam or pressure tactics.
What helps
  • Good communication and follow-through.
  • Connection to a school, college, university or student society.
  • Interest in education and student communities.
  • Comfort sharing feedback honestly, including criticism.

The ambassador programme is being planned. Any certificates, benefits or incentives will be confirmed in writing before the programme opens.
