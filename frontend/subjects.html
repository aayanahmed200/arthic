01 / Medical

For the medical route.

Preparation centred on the science and reasoning skills needed for medical and dental college admission.

Medical & dental

MDCAT

The national medical and dental college admission route. Build a steady foundation across the subjects in the current official syllabus, then use focused practice to find the topics that need more attention.

Preparation focusScience concepts, careful reading and reasoning.

02 / Engineering & computing

Different tests. Different formats.

These routes lead to related fields, but each university sets its own admission process and programme requirements.

UET route

ECAT

An entry-test route used for programmes at UET Lahore and its campuses. Check the current cycle for the subject combination and eligibility attached to your chosen programme.

Preparation focusMathematics and relevant science foundations.

NUST route

NUST NET

NUST's own undergraduate entry test. The subject group depends on the programme; NUST says the test is based primarily on SSC and HSSC studies and uses multiple-choice questions.

FAST-NUCES route

FAST

FAST sets its own admission process. For computing programmes, its 2026 prospectus lists the FAST test, SAT, or NTS NAT-IE / NAT-ICS as test options. Other programmes may have different criteria.

03 / Law

Know which tests go together.

Law admission can involve more than one test. The exact combination matters, so check the university's current instructions.

LUMS BA-LL.B

LUMS Law

For the BA-LL.B programme, LUMS currently lists SAT, ACT or the LUMS Common Admission Test (LCAT), plus the HEC Law Admission Test (LAT). This is not the UK LNAT. Always check the admissions page for the intake you're applying to.

04 / Civil service

For federal and provincial service.

Longer preparation routes that reward subject depth, writing practice and a clear understanding of the examination structure.

Federal

CSS

The Central Superior Services examination is the federal competitive route. Preparation spans compulsory and optional subjects, written expression and the other stages set by the current examination rules.

Preparation focusStructured reading, writing and subject coverage.

Provincial

PMS

Provincial Management Service examinations are administered by the relevant provincial public service commission. Subjects and procedures can differ by province and recruitment cycle.

Preparation focusProvincial syllabus, writing and current affairs.

i

Exam formats, eligibility and admission requirements can change between cycles. Use this page to understand the routes, then confirm current details with the relevant university or examining body. LUMS currently describes its BA-LL.B test combination on its official admissions page; HEC publishes LAT information through its Education Testing Council.
