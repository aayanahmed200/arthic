what each exam actually covers

MDCAT — the entry test for medical and dental college admissions in Pakistan. biology, chemistry, and physics carry most of the weight, alongside English and logical reasoning; arthic's mentor tracks which of those five areas is actually costing you marks, not just your overall score.

ECAT — the entry test used for engineering admissions in Punjab, covering mathematics, physics, and chemistry at the intermediate level.

NUST NET — NUST's own entry test for its engineering, computer science, and business programmes, run separately from ECAT with its own format.

FAST — FAST-NUCES's own admission test for its computer science and engineering programmes, with a different structure again from ECAT and NUST NET.

CSS — the Central Superior Services exam, the federal civil service exam that gives successful candidates one shot a year and covers compulsory and optional subjects plus an essay component.

PMS — the Provincial Management Service exam, the provincial equivalent of CSS, run by each province's own public service commission.

LUMS LNAT — LUMS's own admission test for its law programme at the Shaikh Ahmad Hassan School of Law, testing reading comprehension and argument analysis rather than legal knowledge you're not expected to have yet.
