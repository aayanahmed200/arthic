03 / LAW & UNIVERSITY ADMISSION

Keep the admissions requirements straight.

For LUMS BA-LL.B, applicants may need more than one test. Check the latest LUMS instructions and the relevant HEC test information for your intake.

Important distinctionLUMS BA-LL.B lists SAT, ACT or LCAT, plus the HEC Law Admission Test (LAT). It is not the UK LNAT.

04 / COMPETITIVE EXAMS

Prepare for depth, not just recall.

CSS and PMS require a longer-term routine. Your plan should make space for subject coverage, written expression and the examination rules for the relevant cycle.

CSSFederal competitive examination, with compulsory and optional subjects.
PMSProvincial examination; subjects and process vary by commission.

Always verify before you apply. Formats, eligibility and admission requirements can change between cycles. Use this page as an orientation, then confirm the details with the official university or examining body.
