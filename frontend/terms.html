terms.

Using arthic.tech, joining the waitlist, or applying for a role means you're agreeing to the following. It's short on purpose.

the site

Everything here — the writing, the design, the arthic name and product concept — belongs to arthic unless we say otherwise. You're welcome to link to it, quote it with credit, or talk about it; you're not welcome to republish it as your own or scrape it to train something else without asking first.

the waitlist

Joining the waitlist doesn't create a contract, a guaranteed price, or a promised delivery date — arthic is still in development. It adds your email to a list we'll use to tell you when early access opens and, occasionally, to share progress before then.

job applications

Applying for a role doesn't create any kind of employment relationship, and we can't promise a response time — we're a small team reading every application ourselves. Anything you send us (portfolio links, writing samples, ideas) stays yours; sending it to us doesn't transfer ownership of it to arthic.

about the regulatory information on this site

Pages on arthic.tech reference sustainability-disclosure frameworks — the EU's CSRD, California's SB 253, the ISSB standards, and others — to explain why this product exists. That's context, not legal or compliance advice. These frameworks are actively being revised by regulators, and nothing on this site is a definitive statement of what your company is required to report or by when. Talk to your own counsel or compliance advisor about your specific obligations.

no warranty

The site, and arthic the product once it exists, are provided as-is. We're a small team building something we care about, not a company that can promise zero bugs or uninterrupted uptime. We'll fix what we can, as fast as a small team reasonably can.
