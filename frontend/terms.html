the plain-English rules for using arthic.tech, joining the waitlist, or applying for a role.

Using arthic.tech, joining the waitlist, or applying for a role means you're agreeing to the following. It's short on purpose.

the site

Everything here — the writing, the design, the arthic name and product concept — belongs to arthic unless we say otherwise. You're welcome to link to it, quote it with credit, or talk about it; you're not welcome to republish it as your own or scrape it to train something else without asking first.

the waitlist

Joining the waitlist doesn't create a contract, a guaranteed price, or a promised delivery date — arthic is still in development. It adds your email to a list we'll use to tell you when early access opens and, occasionally, to share progress before then.

job applications

Applying for a role doesn't create any kind of employment relationship, and we can't promise a response time — we're a small team reading every application ourselves. Anything you send us (portfolio links, writing samples, ideas) stays yours; sending it to us doesn't transfer ownership of it to arthic.

about the exam information on this site

arthic references exam formats, weightages, and structures for MDCAT, ECAT, NUST NET, FAST, CSS, PMS, and LUMS LNAT to explain how the platform is built. That's context, not an official statement — the exam bodies and institutions themselves (PMDC, UET, NUST, FAST-NUCES, FPSC, the provincial public service commissions, and LUMS) change formats and dates from year to year, and their own announcements are always the authoritative source, not this site.

about the mentor, once it exists

arthic the AI mentor is a study aid, not a substitute for your own coursework, an academy, or a registered exam-prep provider — and not a guarantee of any particular score or admission outcome. We'll be as accurate as we can, and we'll always tell you when we're not sure, but what you submit on exam day is on you.

no warranty

The site, and arthic the product once it exists, are provided as-is. We're a small team building something we care about, not a company that can promise zero bugs or uninterrupted uptime. We'll fix what we can, as fast as a small team reasonably can.

acceptable use

Don't use the forms on this site to send spam, attempt to break into anything, or submit information that isn't yours. We reserve the right to remove access for anyone who does.

changes

If these terms change in a way that matters, we'll update this page and move the date at the top, the same way we handle the privacy page.
