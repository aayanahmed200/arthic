the story

not a shooter.
not a survival game.

Orbital puts you on a research station in high orbit, sent to work out what happened after the crew stopped answering. It's first-person and story-driven — closer to a mystery you walk through than a place you're meant to survive. There's no one to fight, and nothing keeping score.

It's built to be finished in an evening, not colonized for a hundred hours: no combat, no crafting, no inventory to manage, no open world to clear. Just a station, its silence, and whatever you can piece together about why everyone left before you did.

  • first-person, story-driven
  • a few hours, start to finish
  • no combat, no crafting, no inventory
  • currently in early development
