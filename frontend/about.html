about

one small studio.
based in the united states.

arthic is based mainly in the United States, with the six of us working together the way most small studios do now — online, async, and without much ceremony. We agree on the same handful of things: that games can be made slowly, without ads, and without asking a publisher's permission first.

philosophy

a short list of things
we won't compromise on.

  1. 01

    ideas

    one idea, taken seriously.

    Every project starts as a single question worth spending years on — not a checklist of features.

  2. 02

    money

    no ads. no live-service tricks.

    We don't sell attention. If a game isn't good enough to finish on its own, we don't ship it.

  3. 03

    price

    priced like we remember being broke.

    Full price shouldn't mean choosing between a game and everything else — especially if you're still a student.

  4. 04

    team

    small team, direct decisions.

    No layers of approval between an idea and the build. If it's good, it ships. If it's not, we cut it.

a note on tools

AI is a tool here. Not the product.

Like any small studio, we use ordinary software to make games — engines, editors, and sometimes AI, the same way we use a compiler. It speeds up the boring parts. It never decides what's good — that's still done by hand, one decision at a time, and it's still the only part of the job that actually matters.
