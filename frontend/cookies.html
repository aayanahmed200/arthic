cookie policy.

shorter than most, because there's genuinely less to disclose: arthic doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies at all.

Most cookie policies are long because most sites use a lot of cookies — analytics, advertising, third-party trackers, session management across a dozen services. arthic doesn't do any of that today, so this page says exactly what's true and stops there rather than padding it out to look more official.

1. what arthic actually stores

arthic.tech stores exactly one thing in your browser: your light/dark theme preference, saved via localStorage under the key arthic-theme. That's it. It's not a cookie in the technical sense — it isn't sent to our servers with every request the way a cookie is — and it exists purely so the site remembers which theme you picked instead of resetting every time you visit.

If your browser has localStorage disabled (private browsing modes sometimes do this), the site still works fine — it just falls back to matching your device's own light/dark setting each time instead of remembering a manual choice.

2. what arthic does not use

No analytics cookies. No advertising or retargeting pixels. No third-party trackers. No session cookies for logged-in accounts, because there's no account system live yet. If and when arthic ships features that genuinely need cookies — a real login system, for instance — this page will be updated to say so plainly, and if that ever includes anything beyond strictly-necessary cookies, we'll ask first rather than assume.

3. Arlo, the support chat

The chat widget on this site keeps your conversation in memory for the current page visit only. Refreshing the page starts a new conversation. Nothing from that chat is written to localStorage, a cookie, or anywhere else in your browser.

4. the waitlist and job applications

Submitting the waitlist form or a job application sends that information to arthic's own servers to act on it (see the privacy policy for what happens to it there) — that's a direct request you make by submitting a form, not a cookie or browser-storage mechanism, so it isn't covered by this page.

5. questions

If something on this page stops being accurate — because arthic adds a feature that changes any of this — we'll update it rather than leave a stale policy up. In the meantime, if anything here is unclear, email hello@arthic.tech.
