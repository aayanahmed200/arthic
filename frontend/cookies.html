Cookies and browser storage.

What the Arthic exam-preparation site stores in your browser, and which external requests it makes to display its typefaces.

Most cookie policies are long because most sites use a lot of cookies — analytics, advertising, third-party trackers, session management across a dozen services. Arthic doesn't do any of that today, so this page says exactly what's true and stops there rather than padding it out to look more official.

1. browser storage

The current public site does not save a theme preference or a student profile in localStorage. The sample study preview keeps the active question and answer feedback in the page while you use it; that temporary practice state resets when the page is reloaded. It is not a saved student account or a persistent study history.

2. analytics and advertising

The site does not currently use analytics cookies, advertising or retargeting pixels, or third-party advertising trackers. There is no live student login or account session on the public site. If future account or platform features require additional browser storage, this page will be updated to explain what is used and why before those features are introduced.

3. external font requests

Arthic loads the Inter and Lilita One typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser requests the font files from Google, which may receive your IP address and basic browser request information needed to deliver them. This request provides the site's typefaces; Arthic does not use it for advertising or analytics. The old floating support-chat widget is not part of the current site.

4. the waitlist and job applications

Submitting the waitlist form or a job application sends that information to Arthic's own servers to act on it (see the privacy policy for what happens to it there) — that's a direct request you make by submitting a form, not a cookie or browser-storage mechanism, so it isn't covered by this page.

5. questions

If something on this page stops being accurate — because Arthic adds a feature that changes any of this — we'll update it rather than leave a stale policy up. In the meantime, if anything here is unclear, email hello@arthic.tech.
