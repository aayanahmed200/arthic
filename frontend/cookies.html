Cookies and browser storage.

What the site stores on your device, what is essential to sign in, and what it does not use.

On this page Overview Sign-in session storage Practice and preferences Cookies External services Your controls Contact

A cookie is a small piece of information that a website or service may store in your browser. Browser storage also includes mechanisms such as sessionStorage and localStorage. This notice covers both, because they can serve similar purposes even though they are technically different.

1. Essential sign-in session storage

When you choose Google sign-in and complete account setup, Arthic stores the authentication provider's access and refresh credentials in the browser's sessionStorage for the active tab. This allows the site to make authenticated requests for your account and practice progress and to refresh the session while the tab remains open. The stored credentials are not a tracking cookie. Closing the tab or signing out removes the browser session; signing out also asks the authentication provider to end the session.

These credentials are sensitive. Do not share your device with someone you do not trust, keep your browser up to date, and sign out on a shared computer. If the browser session is cleared, you may need to sign in again. Arthic does not store your Google password.

2. Practice state and preferences

For visitors who are not signed in, an active practice quiz and its answer feedback remain in the page's memory and reset when the page is reloaded or closed. They are not a saved profile. When you are signed in and begin a synced session, question attempts and session totals are sent to Arthic's server and associated with your account so they can appear in the dashboard on another device after you sign in there. Settings you save are stored with your account.

The public website does not currently use localStorage for advertising, behavioural profiling or analytics identifiers. Site fonts are bundled with the website and served as local assets; the current font setup does not require a Google Fonts request.

3. Cookies and analytics

Arthic does not set analytics, advertising or retargeting cookies and does not run third-party analytics or advertising pixels. The site does record selected public-page views using a first-party server-side counter that stores only the page route, date and aggregate count. It does not set an analytics cookie or send a persistent visitor identifier. Those counts are page views, not unique visitors. Our hosting, security and authentication providers may use strictly operational cookies or similar mechanisms on their own domains to deliver their services. When you are redirected to Google to authenticate, Google's own cookies and browser technologies are governed by Google's policies.

We do not use a third-party analytics or advertising cookie that requires an opt-in banner on the current site. If we add non-essential cookies or tracking in the future, we will update this notice and provide the choices required by applicable law before enabling them.

4. External services

Google is used only when you actively choose the Google sign-in option. Supabase provides authentication and may provide the database; Vercel hosts and serves Arthic. These services may process technical information needed to provide authentication, hosting, storage and security. Review their own policies for details of their processing.

5. Your controls

You can clear browser site data through your browser settings, close the tab to clear its sessionStorage, or sign out from Arthic Settings. Clearing browser data does not itself delete practice records already saved on Arthic's servers. To request server-side access, export or deletion, use the account controls when available or email hello@arthic.tech.

Blocking all storage may prevent sign-in or synced account features from working. The public pages and anonymous sample practice can still be used without creating an account, subject to ordinary browser and security requirements.

6. Contact and updates

If we change the browser technologies used by Arthic, we will update this notice and its date. Questions can be sent to hello@arthic.tech.

This notice describes the current intended browser-storage setup. Your browser, Google and other third-party services may apply their own storage controls and policies.
