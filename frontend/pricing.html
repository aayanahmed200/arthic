QuestionsPricing and access

A few things worth knowing.

Can I buy a subscription now?
No. Arthic is in development and paid subscriptions are not open. We will announce availability before requesting payment.
Will early access be free?
The current plan is to offer invited early-access students access without charge. The invitation details and included features will be stated clearly before you accept.
Is Rs. 8,999 the final Plus price?
No. It is a proposed monthly price, not a final or purchasable offer. We will confirm any paid plan and its terms before launch.
Does joining the list create an account?
No. The early-access form is an email interest list. It does not create a product account or activate a paid service.
