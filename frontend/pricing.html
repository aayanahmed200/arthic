LIVE TUTORING

One-to-one and group session rates

A Pakistan 2026 tutoring guide lists online individual tuition at about Rs. 800–1,500/hour for Matric subjects and Rs. 1,500–3,000/hour for O/A-Level subjects. It places 3–5 student group sessions around Rs. 400–700 per student per session. These are live teaching rates, not monthly software subscriptions.

QuestionsPlans and access

Before you choose.

Can I subscribe now?

No. These are proposed launch prices only. There is no checkout and no payment is being collected.

How does the group plan work?

It is intended for at least five students studying the same track or preparing together. The group tools and final minimum size will be confirmed before launch.

Is coaching included in the subscription?

No. Coaching is proposed as a separately priced, guided session. Mentor selection, availability and booking are not live yet.

Are these final prices?

No. They are planning figures in Pakistani rupees. Any final price and plan terms will be displayed before a student is asked to pay.
