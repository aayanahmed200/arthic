why free right now

we're not charging for something that isn't finished.

arthic is still early — new features ship every week, and the mentor gets better the more students actually use it and tell us where it's wrong. charging before it's ready would mean charging you to help us test it. early access stays free so the people helping us build this get the most out of it, not the least.

questions

frequently asked

which exams does arthic actually cover?
MDCAT, ECAT, NUST NET, the FAST entry test, CSS, PMS, and LUMS LNAT today. we're a small team prioritizing depth over breadth — one track done properly beats seven done halfway, so we're expanding carefully rather than all at once.
is arthic free right now?
yes — full access to the mentor is free during early access, on one exam track at a time. that's not an introductory discount; it's genuinely free while we're building.
does arthic replace an academy or a tutor?
not exactly. arthic is built to be there at 1am when your academy's closed and you're stuck on one question — most students use it alongside a coaching program, not instead of one.
do you support Urdu?
not yet. arthic is English-only while we're early, so the mentor and the rest of the product get our full attention before we split focus. full Urdu support — real script, not a transliteration shortcut — is on the roadmap; join the waitlist and we'll tell you the moment it ships.
what happens to the price after early access?
Plus is planned at Rs. 8,999/month once early access ends — but that's today's plan, not a locked-in number. we'll confirm it with feedback from the students actually using it first. free access stays free for one exam track either way.
