why arthic

most exam prep here asks you to memorize.
arthic asks you to understand.

MDCAT, ECAT, NUST NET, FAST, CSS, PMS, and LUMS LNAT are some of the most competitive exams in the country, and most prep for them still means the same thing: a stack of guides, a crowded academy, and an instructor who doesn't have time to explain a concept twice. If English isn't the language you think fastest in, that gap gets wider, not smaller.

arthic is an all-in-one platform that meets you where you actually are — an AI mentor that teaches in English and Urdu in the same conversation, past papers organized by topic instead of dumped in one PDF, a study plan personalized to your exam date, and an exam calendar so you never miss a deadline. built to notice when you're pattern-matching instead of understanding, and to slow down exactly there instead of moving on to the next chapter.
