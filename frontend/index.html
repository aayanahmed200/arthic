why now

the rules didn't slow down.
most teams' spreadsheets did.

Two of the largest sustainability-disclosure regimes in the world are landing on mid-sized companies at once. The EU's CSRD is pulling large and mid-market companies into standardized ESRS reporting; California's SB 253 requires large companies doing business in the state to disclose their emissions. Neither has stayed still — thresholds, timelines, and scope have all shifted more than once in the past two years, which makes "wait and see what the final rule says" the most expensive strategy on the table.

arthic doesn't bet on any one version of the rules staying put. It tracks what's actually in force, maps your data to it, and keeps your report current when the rules move — because at this rate, they will.

  • CSRD (EU) — ESRS-aligned reporting
  • SB 253 (California) — Scope 1–3 emissions
  • ISSB / IFRS S1 & S2 — the emerging global baseline
  • more added as frameworks are finalized, not guessed at early

your report shouldn't be a fire drill every quarter.

how it works

four steps.
no spreadsheet archaeology.

  1. 01

    connect

    bring your data as it already exists

    Plug in the spreadsheets, utility bills, and systems you already use. arthic doesn't require a clean data warehouse to start — most mid-sized companies don't have one, and we're not waiting for you to build one first.

  2. 02

    map

    map it to every framework that applies

    One import, mapped against CSRD's ESRS, SB 253, and ISSB at the same time — instead of re-entering the same numbers into three different templates for three different regulators.

  3. 03

    report

    generate an audit-ready report

    A structured, sourced report with every figure traceable back to where it came from — built for an auditor to read, not just a board deck to skim.

  4. 04

    improve

    get guidance, not just a grade

    arthic's AI reads your numbers and flags the highest-leverage places to invest next — the sustainable-finance layer sitting on top of the compliance layer, not a separate tool you have to reconcile by hand.
