Good to know

Questions, answered.

Can I use Arthic today?

The sample practice session is available to try. The wider question banks, planner and student accounts are still in development.

Which exams is Arthic being built for?

The current focus is MDCAT, ECAT, NUST NET, FAST, LUMS BA-LL.B, CSS and PMS.

Does joining the waitlist create an account?

No. The waitlist records your interest in early access. It does not create a student account or unlock unfinished features.

Will the sample questions be official past papers?

The questions in the current preview are original demonstration questions, not released or official past-paper questions.
