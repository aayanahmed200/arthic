why arthic

most exam prep here asks you to memorize.

how it works

the rule of three.
no shortcuts, no wasted turns.

  1. 01

    identify

    find the actual mistake, not just the wrong answer

    most students re-read the same explanation when they get something wrong. arthic starts by figuring out exactly where your understanding broke — a formula, a definition, a step in the logic — instead of just marking it incorrect.

  2. 02

    explain

    a concept explained the way a friend would

    every concept gets a plain-language explanation and a local, relatable example — in English or Roman Urdu, whichever you're mid-sentence in — until it actually clicks, not just until you nod along.

    SN2 → picked the wrong nucleophile
    ↓ explained with a local example
    rickshaw = SN1
    shared van = SN2
    concept locked in
  3. 03

    test

    a next challenge, not just a pat on the back

    once a concept clicks, arthic doesn't just move to the next topic — it gives you one more question built to test whether it actually stuck, calibrated to be just hard enough.
